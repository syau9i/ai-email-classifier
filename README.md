# AI Email Classifier

## What it does

Automatically analyzes incoming emails
and classifies them into categories.

## Workflow

Gmail
  ↓
n8n
  ↓
AI
  ↓
Classification
  ↓
Google Sheets

## Technologies

- n8n
- OpenAI API
- Gmail
- Google Sheets

## Example

Customer email
        ↓
AI analyzes email
        ↓
Category: Sales
        ↓
Saved to Google Sheets
